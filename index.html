What is SEO and can it help small businesses?

SEO stands for search engine optimization. It’s a way of tuning your website that makes it easier to rank high in search results and be more easily discovered by customers who need your service. Search engines consider your website’s structure, content, and technical health- all of which we optimize so that your business reaches the right customers. If your website has strong SEO, it’s much more likely to rank higher than a competitor whose site may be less SEO optimized. About 2 in every 3 businesses have had some kind of SEO work done.
